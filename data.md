# Data

## Dataset Availability

The datasets used in this repository originate from the **Data Manipulation in Alteryx** course on **DataCamp** and are subject to the copyright and licensing terms of their respective owners.

To respect intellectual property rights and course licensing agreements, the original data files are **not included** in this repository and cannot be redistributed.

This repository contains only the workflows, solutions, and documentation developed as part of the course exercises. Users interested in accessing the datasets should obtain them directly through the official course on DataCamp.

For educational purposes, you may use your own data or the datasets provided through your authorized access to the course.
